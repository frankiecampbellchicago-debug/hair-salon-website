Our Story

A neighborhood salon built on trust, craft, and genuinely great hair days.

Since 2015

Ten years of great hair days

Lumière opened its doors with one goal: make every client feel confident walking out the door. What started as a two-chair studio has grown into a full team of stylists and colorists, but the mission hasn't changed — listen first, then create.

We believe healthy hair and honest advice go further than any trend. That's why every consultation starts with a real conversation about your hair, your lifestyle, and what actually works for you.
